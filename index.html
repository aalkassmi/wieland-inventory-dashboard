Definitions. Views = listing page views on premiertruck.com. From Ads = share of those views that came from a Google Ads click. Leads = people who sent a check availability or photo request form on that truck. Calls = phone taps on the listing. Status = on the site today (Motive). Price and days on lot shown for listed units only; "No price" = listed on the site with no price showing.

Flags. Red days = 180+ on the lot; bold = 90 to 179. Units that sold or left the site during the window keep their activity but show no price or age. ↻ = transferred between stores in this region; days shown are total days in the region, since the store-level age restarts at zero on a transfer.

Locations. A listed truck sits under the store that owns it in Motive, with its activity combined across all five stores. A truck that left the site sits under the store where it got the most views.
